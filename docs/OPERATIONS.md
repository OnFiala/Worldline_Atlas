# Bounded research procedure

This is a method specification, not an installed automation. The local demo has no researcher, scheduler or external account access.

1. Search existing observations, mechanisms, forecasts and prior mistakes. Choose a finite question and work allowance.
2. Gather public sources across regions and disciplines; deduplicate releases and syndications by origin family. Record actual access limits.
3. Verify decisive numbers and claims against primary evidence. Keep observation periods, publication dates and check times distinct.
4. Form a conditional mechanism. Record scale, region, direct and downstream effects, lag, assumptions, alternative explanations and tests that could refute it.
5. Prioritize correction of material errors and due evaluations, then well-supported consequential exposures, then verification of uncertain signals. Keep three active prospective tests and ten queued candidates at most.
6. Prepare a bounded versioned proposal. A separately authenticated integrator must reconcile current versions before importing it; the public demo provides no HTTP import endpoint.
7. Read back changed identities and preserved history. Append outcomes only after their target period and with the predeclared evidence. Retain failures and ambiguities.

Model selection, cost budgets and schedules would belong to a future runner. Do not infer them from the availability of an API. Do not create accounts, service credentials or public deployments as part of a research update.
